My Approach

I am someone who gets excited about solving hard problems with machine learning. I do not just train models, I build complete systems that work in production. That means thinking about everything from data pipelines and feature engineering to model monitoring and A/B testing.

I have spent years working on neural architectures, optimizing hyperparameters, and debugging why models fail in the real world. I know that getting a model to 95% accuracy is one thing, making it reliable in production is another thing entirely.

What I enjoy most is the moment when research becomes reality. When you see your model making predictions on real data, helping real users, that is what makes all the hyperparameter tuning worth it.
